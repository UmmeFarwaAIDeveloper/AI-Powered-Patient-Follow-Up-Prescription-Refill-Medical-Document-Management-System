# 🏥 AI-Powered Patient Follow-Up, Prescription Refill & Medical Document Management System

An intelligent healthcare automation system built with n8n that helps clinics, medical practices, and hospitals automate patient follow-ups, prescription refill requests, and medical document processing while reducing manual administrative work.


# 📌 Problem Statement

Healthcare providers handle hundreds of repetitive operational tasks every week.

Common challenges include:

- Patients forgetting to follow up after receiving lab results
- Staff spending hours handling prescription refill requests
- Manual processing of referrals, test reports, and insurance documents
- Delayed communication between patients, doctors, and departments
- Administrative teams overwhelmed by routine tasks

These issues increase workload, create delays in patient care, and reduce operational efficiency.


# ✅ Solution

This system automates key healthcare workflows to ensure patients receive timely communication while reducing manual effort for healthcare staff.

The platform focuses on three critical areas:

### 1. Patient Follow-Up Automation
### 2. Prescription Refill Management
### 3. Medical Document Processing


# 🚀 Workflow 1: Patient Follow-Up System

## Problem

Patients often receive lab results but fail to schedule follow-up appointments.

This can lead to:

- Missed treatment opportunities
- Delayed care
- Increased administrative calls

## Workflow

```text
Lab Report Available
        ↓
Patient Notified
        ↓
Doctor Alerted
        ↓
Follow-Up Appointment Suggested
        ↓
Booking Link Sent
        ↓
Patient Response Tracked
```

## Benefits

✅ Improved patient engagement

✅ Better continuity of care

✅ Faster follow-up appointments

✅ Reduced manual outreach


# 💊 Workflow 2: Prescription Refill Management

## Problem

Patients often forget medication refills.

Healthcare staff spend significant time handling refill requests manually.

## Workflow

text
Prescription Near Expiry
          ↓
Patient Reminder
          ↓
Refill Request Submitted
          ↓
Doctor Review & Approval
          ↓
Pharmacy Notified
          ↓
Patient Updated
```

## Benefits

✅ Reduced administrative calls

✅ Improved medication adherence

✅ Faster refill approvals

✅ Better patient experience


# 📄 Workflow 3: Medical Document Processing

## Problem

Healthcare organizations receive large volumes of:

- Referrals
- Test Reports
- Insurance Forms
- Medical Documents

Sorting and routing documents manually takes time and introduces errors.

## Workflow

text
Document Uploaded
        ↓
AI Reads Document
        ↓
Document Categorized
        ↓
Patient Record Updated
        ↓
Correct Department Notified
```

## Benefits

✅ Faster document processing

✅ Less manual sorting

✅ Improved record accuracy

✅ Better department coordination


# 🧠 AI Capabilities

The system uses AI to:

- Classify uploaded documents
- Extract important information from medical files
- Categorize reports automatically
- Route information to the correct workflow
- Support patient communication processes

**Note:** AI supports operational workflows and does not provide medical diagnoses.
